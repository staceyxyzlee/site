slumberparty

San Mateo, 2024-present

slumberparty was a 2v2 house & wh/aacking battle hosted by Str8jacket meant to foster community and celebrate the styles' shared club history. I was tasked with creating promotional materials to be shared digitally, including an event flyer and judge announcements, as well as printed awards.


I worked to balance the dichomoty of the two differing dance styles, focusing on their underground roots and reconstructing the idea of a slumber party, turning a sleepy, cozy activity into a lively dance party. The approach was ironic and brutalist, combining colorful imagery with bold, graphic elements.

flyer


Initial concepts explored a range of experiences the flyer could convey. In the end, the concept chosen best mirrored the high energy we hoped to achieve come event night, steering away from stereotypical motifs that read as relaxed.

rough ideas pulled from pitch deck


I created a series of awards which were presented at the event. Each award played off the initial flyer, subverting original motifs and imagery to fit each individual accomplishment.
